# ColonyGames Official Sports Rulebook & Regulations

## 1. Box Cricket Championship
- **Format**: 6-a-side tennis ball box cricket, 6 overs per innings.
- **Eligibility**: Open to all colony residents aged 14 and above.
- **Rules**:
  - Maximum 2 overs per bowler.
  - Direct hit out of society net boundary is declared out.
  - Underarm bowling allowed only in senior division.

## 2. Society Football League
- **Format**: 5-a-side rink football with rolling substitutions.
- **Eligibility**: Age 12 to 45 years.
- **Duration**: 20 minutes (two 10-minute halves).
- **Rules**:
  - No slide tackling on turf surface.
  - Goalkeeper must throw the ball underhand.

## 3. Racket Badminton Tournament
- **Categories**: Men's Singles, Women's Singles, Mixed Doubles, Kids (Under 14).
- **Format**: Best of 3 sets of 15 rally points (BWF regulations).
- **Equipment**: Yonex Mavis 350 nylon shuttlecocks provided.

## 4. Slow Cycling Challenge
- **Objective**: Cover a 25-meter marked lane in the longest possible time.
- **Rules**:
  - Both feet must remain on pedals; grounding a foot results in immediate DQ.
  - Wheels must continuously move forward; stopping or turning backwards is not permitted.
  - Open to all age groups (Kids, Teens, Adults, Seniors).

## 5. Table Tennis Championship
- **Categories**: Juniors (Under 16), Open (Any age), Veteran (50+).
- **Format**: 11-point sets, best of 5 games. Standard ITTF service rules apply.

## 6. Society 100m & 400m Dash
- **Categories**: Under 10, Under 16, Open Men, Open Women, Senior Walkathon (60+).
- **Track**: Colony central boulevard paved track with safety lane markers.

## General Tournament Conduct
- All participants must wear their allocated ColonyGames athletic t-shirts and sports shoes.
- Decisions by the Sports Welfare Committee and referees are final and binding.
- Sportsmanship and friendly neighborhood spirit must be upheld at all times.
