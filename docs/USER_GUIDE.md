# ColonyGames User & Administrator Guide

## Resident Guide: How to Register for the Fest

### Step 1: Head of Family Contact
Enter the primary contact person's name, email, and 10-digit mobile number. WhatsApp updates and PDF passes will be sent here.

### Step 2: Colony Address
Choose your Block/Tower (Tower A through F, or Villas) and enter your flat number for society household verification.

### Step 3: Add Family Members
Click **+ Add Member** to register participants. Specify their age, relationship, and official t-shirt size for sports jerseys.

### Step 4: Sports Selection
Assign members to sports based on age eligibility. Each participant can participate in up to 3 individual events and 2 team events.

### Step 5: Terms & Waiver
Review the safety rules, first aid guidelines, and sportsmanship pledge.

### Step 6: Instant Digital Pass
Once registered, download your personalized PDF entry pass containing your QR code, or share it directly to WhatsApp.

## Administrator Guide: Running Fest Operations

### 1. Gate Entry & Pass Scanner
- Open `/admin` on any mobile device or tablet.
- Switch to the **QR Check-in** tab.
- Point the camera at the resident's printed pass or digital screen.
- The scanner will instantly verify ticket authenticity and mark the family as "Checked-In" with a green confirmation banner.

### 2. Live Capacity & Event Management
- Monitor registration caps in real time.
- Edit match venues or adjust game times directly from the Events table.

### 3. Medal Recording & Leaderboard
- At the conclusion of each event, open the Results module to record Gold, Silver, and Bronze winners.
- Tower points are automatically updated on the public `/results` podium.
